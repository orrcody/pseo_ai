Non-Title 13 or Title 26 data stored here. These data were compiled and manipulated from public sources and were not reviewed by the U.S. Census Bureau for disclosure.

## List of files
- fod_aix.csv
  - Contains a series of AI exposure measure for American Community Survey fields of degree. "Eloundou_mean" refers to the Eloundou et al. (2024) GPT-4 Beta measure. "Handa_mean" refers to the Handa et al. (2025) observed useage rates. "Handa_auto_mean" is the share of tasks that Handa et al. classify as automative. "Eisfeldt" refers to the Eisfeldt et al. (2023) Core Task Exposure score. "Occ_top5_mean" is the share of a major's occupations in the top quintile of the GPT-4 Beta occupation-level distribution.
  - The "std" suffix denotes standardized.
  - Large double majors are included.
